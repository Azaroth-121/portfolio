## Problem

I needed to pass a specific calculus course while working full time. Reading
the textbook and grinding practice problems on paper wasn't making the material
stick, and I didn't have time to waste on a study method that wasn't working.

## Approach

I built an exam-prep app for that exact course instead. Structuring the
material well enough to build a tool that teaches it forced me to actually
understand it — the building was the studying, not a side effect of it.

<MediaPlaceholder label="App screenshot" />

## Architecture

- React front end, built as a progressive web app
- Offline-first: works without a connection once loaded, so it's usable
  anywhere, including places with unreliable internet
- Content structured around the course's actual exam format, not a generic
  curriculum

## Result

A working, installable exam-prep PWA, live at the link above.

## What I'd do differently

I'd add spaced-repetition scheduling for the practice problems instead of
letting the learner pick what to review — the parts I under-reviewed on my own
were exactly the parts that needed more repetition.
