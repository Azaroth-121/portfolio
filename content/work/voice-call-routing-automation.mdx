## Problem

Inbound calls were being triaged manually — someone had to answer, figure out
intent, and either route the call or take down details by hand. That doesn't
scale, and it drops information between the call and the CRM.

## Approach

I mapped the manual process first: what a human dispatcher actually listened
for, how they decided where a call went, and what they wrote down afterward.
Only once that process was well understood did I automate it — encoding a
process nobody had run manually yet would have meant automating guesses.

## Architecture

- Telephony API integration for inbound call handling
- Intent capture logic to classify the reason for the call
- Routing rules that send the call to the right destination or queue based on
  captured intent
- Scheduling logic for calls that resolve to a booking
- Automated CRM logging, so every call leaves a record
  without manual entry

<MediaPlaceholder label="CRM record example" />

## Result

Calls are captured, classified, routed, and logged into the CRM automatically,
replacing a manual dispatch step and removing the gap between "the call
happened" and "it's in the system."

## What I'd do differently

I'd build the intent-classification confidence threshold in from day one rather
than adding it after the fact — low-confidence calls should fall back to a
human immediately instead of being routed on a best guess.
